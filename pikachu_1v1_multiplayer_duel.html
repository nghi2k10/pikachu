Onet Pikachu Duel Guide

1. Connecting Rule (Onet 3-Line Path):

You can connect two matching tiles if the line connecting them turns at most 2 times (consists of up to 3 orthogonal line segments) without crossing any other obstacle tile. Empty outer space outside the board can also be used as a passage!

2. Multiplayer Duel Modes:

  • Split Race (Same Seed): Both players start with the identical board layout side-by-side. The first to clear their board or whoever has the most points when the timer expires wins!
  • PvP Combos & Sabotage: Match tiles quickly! Every 3 combos fires a freeze or smokebomb attack at your rival.
  • Shared Battleground: 1 single board shared in the center. Both players rush to snatch pairs from the same grid!

Architecture Note (Online 1v1 Feasibility):

For online play across separate devices, the exact same seed generator and deterministic BFS validator used here can be hosted on a Node.js / WebSocket server (or WebRTC PeerJS). Client sends `(r1, c1, r2, c2)`, server validates BFS and broadcasts sync events.
